# monday-morning — kandidat & seleksi pairwise

Kriteria juri (stand-up comic 20 tahun), urutan penting:
1. HARUS tentang Senin pagi (bukan hari/topik lain)
2. Ketawa nyata, ada turn/punchline — bukan sekadar keluhan Senin
3. Kejutan — twist bukan hal pertama yang dipikirkan orang
4. Sedikit kata, tiap kata bekerja, setup tidak mubazir
5. Segar — bukan klise Senin yang itu-itu saja (alarm/snooze/kopi = kalah di 3 & 5)

## 14 kandidat (semua menghindari alarm/snooze/kopi)

1. Monday showed up at my door at 6 a.m. with a clipboard. Apparently I owe it five days, and it's collecting all of them this week.
2. Monday is the only coworker who never takes a vacation and somehow still ruins mine.
3. I put Monday on Do Not Disturb. It left a voicemail. It's 47 meetings long.
4. Monday morning: my calendar looked at me the way a dentist looks at a tooth he's been saving for last.
5. Monday morning isn't a day. It's a jump scare with a recurring calendar invite.
6. My houseplants lean toward the window on Mondays. Even they want out.
7. I asked Monday for a lighter schedule. It sent me a 9 a.m. meeting about the 9 a.m. meeting.
8. Monday morning my fridge hums in a minor key. It's been rehearsing this all weekend.
9. Monday is just Sunday's leftovers, reheated by someone who hates me.
10. My Monday motivation and I are in a long-distance relationship. It lives on Friday.
11. Monday knocked, and my weekend hid in the closet pretending to be a Tuesday.
12. I tried to unsubscribe from Mondays. The confirmation email arrives next Monday.
13. Monday morning: the sun clocked in, looked around, and asked if it could work from home.
14. Monday is the sequel nobody asked for, released every week without a trailer.

## Seleksi pairwise (lawan kriteria di atas)

- #1 vs #2: #1 menang di 3 (Monday sebagai debt collector = sudut tak umum), #2 "coworker" sudah sering dipakai. #1 lanjut.
- #3 vs #7: #3 menang di 4 (lebih pendek) dan angka spesifik "47 meetings" mendarat lebih tajam daripada loop meeting-meeting #7. #3 lanjut.
- #4 vs #8: #4 menang di 2 (gambar dentist jelas), #8 terlalu puitis, turn-nya lemah. #4 lanjut.
- #6 vs #13: #13 menang di 3 (matahari WFH lebih tak terduga daripada tanaman), #6 tipis. #13 lanjut.
- #9 vs #10: #9 menang di 4 & 5 (leftovers reheated ringkas + segar), #10 konsep "long-distance relationship" sudah klise format joke. #9 lanjut.
- #11 vs #12: #12 menang di 2 (punchline "arrives next Monday" menutup loop), #11 setup-nya lucu tapi turn-nya datar. #12 lanjut.
- #14 vs #5: #5 menang di 3 & 5 — "jump scare" untuk Senin pagi belum jadi klise, dan "recurring calendar invite" adalah detail spesifik yang cuma masuk akal untuk Senin. #14 "sequel" formatnya generik. #5 lanjut.
- Semifinal #1 vs #3: #3 menang di 4 (jauh lebih hemat kata) dengan turn sama kuatnya. #3 lanjut.
- Semifinal #4 vs #13: #4 menang di 2 (punchline dentist lebih mendarat), #13 lebih "cute" daripada lucu. #4 lanjut.
- Semifinal #9 vs #12: #12 menang di 3 (twist loop unsubscribe lebih mengejutkan), #9 masih terasa keluhan berbalut metafora. #12 lanjut.
- Semifinal #5 vs #3: #5 menang di 5 (jump scare + recurring invite = kombinasi paling segar di daftar) dan di 4 (satu kalimat pendek). #3 kuat tapi format "Do Not Disturb / voicemail" lebih dekat ke joke HP generik. #5 lanjut.
- Semifinal #4 vs #12: #4 menang di 2 & 4 — gambar dentist sekali baca langsung kena, #12 butuh dua langkah logika (unsubscribe → email konfirmasi). #4 lanjut.
- Final #5 vs #4: #5 menang di 3, 4, 5. Twist jump-scare bukan hal pertama yang dipikirkan orang soal Senin, wording paling ketat (punchline di kata terakhir), dan tidak menyentuh klise alarm/snooze/kopi sama sekali. #4 bagus tapi lebih panjang dan metafora dentist agak jauh dari "pagi".

## Pemenang

> Monday morning isn't a day. It's a jump scare with a recurring calendar invite.

Alasan: kriteria 1 eksplisit ("Monday morning"), kriteria 2 turn di kalimat kedua, kriteria 3 jump scare = kejutan, kriteria 4 dua kalimat pendek tanpa kata mubazir, kriteria 5 sudut kalender/invite belum klise.
